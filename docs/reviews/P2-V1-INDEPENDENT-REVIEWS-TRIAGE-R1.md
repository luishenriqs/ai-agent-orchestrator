# AI Agent Orchestrator — Consolidated Independent Review Triage (P2-R1)

## 1. Sources and method

This triage uses both complete P2-R1 reviews supplied in the session. **AGY** is the Gemini 4 Neon review run through Antigravity. **CODEX** is the separate Codex review. Both issued `CHANGES REQUIRED`. AGY reported 3 BLOCKER, 5 MAJOR, 4 MINOR, and 1 EDITORIAL finding. CODEX reported 5 BLOCKER, 7 MAJOR, 3 MINOR, and 1 EDITORIAL finding. Each prefixed ID retains the original review identifier. Overlaps below were determined by comparing the counterexamples and requested guarantees. The original severities are retained.

- [P2-R1 candidate](../specifications/P2-V1-OPERATIONAL-CONTRACTS-R1.md) and [review protocol](P2-V1-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-PROMPT-R1.md).
- [Homologated v0.4 architecture](../architecture/003-diretrizes-arquiteturais-v04.md) and [formal approval record](../architecture/004-registro-homologacao-v04.md). The architecture digest matched `6e6a8987f112d866c4f52cd2d16c60b33548a6542c5299b510036d4efedc409b`.
- [P2-R2 candidate](../specifications/P2-V1-OPERATIONAL-CONTRACTS-R2.md). Consolidation began at `main` HEAD `fffe62a7ec498dac7b093c0a3d182537fba188d4`. P1 decisions are recorded in R1, the protocol, and the human instructions for this revision.

**Classification.** Substantiated findings identify a contract gap or an actual feasibility risk. Partial findings identify a gap while proposing a remedy broader than required or combining required and optional changes. Optional recommendations address proportionality, validation design, or editorial clarity. **Addressed** denotes a text change subject to independent regression review. **P3 proof pending** denotes an empirical obligation. References such as `AGY-003` and `CODEX-003` in the overlap column abbreviate the full IDs used in the first column.

## 4. Disposition and residual work

The R2 text addresses the substantiated contract findings. The partially substantiated findings retain their demonstrated safety and coverage requirements. Cleanup may use verified equivalent protected recovery material under specific authorization. A before-and-after digest by itself cannot establish that test inputs were stable while in use. Real push or PR validation and cleanup are conditional extensions to VR-01.

P3 proof obligations remain for the human authority boundary, process ownership and descendant containment on restart, local durability under failure, Git/GitHub capability isolation and remote preconditions, and reliable capture and evidence attribution. The tests in R2 are future acceptance criteria. Human homologation and authorization to begin P3 remain separate decisions.
