## 4. Deliberately open questions vs. documentation defects

In accordance with `decisoes-delegacao.md` (temporary copy of the human delegation) (L18), the 16 topics deliberately left open in section 15 (L105–114) **are not document defects**. They are language, framework, persistence/database, final event formats, state names, API/CLI contracts, directories, web interface, retention, subprocesses, sandbox, timeouts/limits, automatic retry, external effects, authentication/authorization, and protocol versioning. Leaving these questions open is consistent with `DEC-01` and avoids premature decisions.

Findings `ARQ-R1-001` through `ARQ-R1-007` point to **documentation consolidation defects**, including reversal of authority on resumption, a control gap in the initial workflow, omissions in the planning handoff, imprecision about the POCs, and weakening of prohibitions already decided by the human owner.

## 5. Conclusion, verdict, and open items

**Verdict** — **`AJUSTES NECESSÁRIOS`** (adjustments required). The candidate version is concise, disciplined about simplicity, and faithful to most of the 16 decisions, with no `BLOCKER` findings. However, the **3 `MAJOR` findings** preclude recommending immediate formal approval.

**Required before formal human approval**
1. Correct `ARQ-R1-001` (section 11, orchestrator authority on resumption, unexpected restarts, and escalation to the human in the event of material uncertainty).
2. Correct `ARQ-R1-002` (section 6, step 1, prior registration and validation of the authorized task in the orchestrator before coordinated actions).
3. Correct `ARQ-R1-003` (sections 15 and 16, incorporate `DEC-15`, `DEC-14`, and `DEC-03` into the planning scope and sufficiency criteria).

**Recommended additional remediation** — Address `ARQ-R1-004` through `ARQ-R1-007` in the same cycle, followed by a brief regression review (R2) and submission to the formal human approval gate.
